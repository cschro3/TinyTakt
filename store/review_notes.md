# Review Notes (Apple / Google)

TinyTakt is a visual countdown timer for children. No account or login needed. No ads, no analytics, no external links in the child area.

**Parental gate:** Tapping the lock icon, any locked (Pro) item, or stopping a running timer while the child-mode lock is on shows a gate: a spoken-number addition question (e.g. "What is seventeen plus eight?") or the parent PIN if set. The purchase screen is only reachable after passing the gate. To review: tap the lock icon → answer the question (the numbers are written as words) → "TinyTakt Pro".

**In-App Purchase:** One non-consumable, `tinytakt_pro_lifetime` (one-time, no subscription). "Restore purchase" is on the Pro screen.

**Notifications:** Used only to signal that a timer has ended while the app is closed (local notification; permission requested from the parents' onboarding step).

**Photos:** Pro users can pick a photo via the system photo picker; stored locally only.

**Apple Music (optional, iOS only):** Parents can connect Apple Music via MusicKit inside the parents area (behind the parental gate) and choose one song as timer music. No search or sign-in is available to children; explicit songs are filtered; no data is collected by the app. To review: lock icon → answer the gate question → "Apple Music" → connect (requires an Apple Music subscription on the review device).
