## Description
How long is ten minutes? Young children find it hard to grasp. TinyTakt makes time visible: a ring around a favourite friend shrinks, a lion walks to the goal, a glass slowly empties – until time is up.

**For everyday routines:** brushing teeth, getting dressed, tidying up, playtime, reading, homework, screen time, kindergarten and school.

**What’s inside**
• Big visual timer with ring, adventure path or fill level
• 9 friendly characters and 5 worlds free, any length from 1 second to hours, quick picks 1–30 minutes
• Gentle music and several alarm sounds, optional 3-2-1 countdown and spoken message
• Child mode: big buttons, accidental changes prevented; stopping needs the parental gate
• Calm mode without motion and music
• Notification when the timer ends, even if the app is closed*
• Works fully offline

**Privacy:** No ads, no tracking, no sign-up. All data stays on the device.

**Free & Pro:** The app stays free to use. The one-time “Pro” purchase (no subscription) unlocks all 46 characters, all worlds, rainbow and hourglass timers, extra music and alarms, your own photos, custom templates and several child profiles. Purchases are only possible behind a parental gate. A purchase applies per platform (iOS/Android) and store account.

*Notification delivery depends on the operating system (e.g. battery saver, Do Not Disturb) and cannot be guaranteed.

## Privacy summary
TinyTakt collects and shares no data. Settings, profiles (nicknames) and optional photos are stored locally only.

## Parent FAQ
**Is it really ad-free?** Yes – no ads, tracking or analytics.
**How are settings and purchases protected?** A spoken-number maths question guards the parents area; an optional PIN.
**Does the timer ring when the app is closed?** Via a system notification if allowed; timing and delivery may be affected by the device.
**Does Pro work on iPhone and Android?** No, purchases apply per platform and store account. “Restore purchase” is in the Pro area.
**Are photos uploaded?** No. They stay local and can be deleted.
